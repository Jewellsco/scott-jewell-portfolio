The problem worth fixing

Registering and paying takes five tools. It should take one.

The program runs great events. The friction is in the sign-up. Today a coach bounces between a tournament site, an age verification site, and five separate payment apps, and the office chases money by hand. Every extra step is a team that almost registered.

Today

  • 1Register on a separate tournament site
  • 2Verify age and grade on a different site
  • 3Pay by Venmo, Cash App, Zelle, Apple Pay, or wait days for a Square invoice
  • 4Hope the receipt email arrives so you know it worked
  • 5Office reconciles payments by hand

What I would change

  • ✓Pick the tourney and add your team in one place
  • ✓Verify age and grade inline, no site hop
  • ✓Pay once by card or Apple Pay, receipt instant
  • ✓Spot open divisions filling in real time
  • ✓Money lands reconciled, no manual chasing

The case for the redesign

Design that pays for itself.

Illustrative, not a promise. The point is the direction. A smoother funnel and instant collection move the numbers that matter to a youth sports program.

Fewer drop offs

Finish the sign-up

Every removed step is a team that finishes registering instead of abandoning midway across sites and apps. Fewer abandoned sign-ups is the fastest revenue there is.

Paid on day one

Collect at registration

Card and Apple Pay at checkout replaces chasing Venmo and mailing Square invoices, so cash arrives sooner and less of it slips.

New line items

Sell tickets online

Passes and spectator wristbands sold inside the same checkout, an upsell the current flow never offers.
